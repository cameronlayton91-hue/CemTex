COMMERCIAL LEADERSHIP

Strategic Brief

My view of the opportunity is straightforward: protect the existing business, understand where profitable growth can actually come from, and build a commercial system that allows multiple brands, products and salespeople to work together.

My operating philosophy: I do not believe sales leadership is simply managing a rep count. The job is to create clarity, identify the highest-value growth opportunities, develop people, improve execution and build a system that makes the entire team more productive.
PLATFORM VIEW

Think Across the System

The opportunity becomes larger when the brands are viewed as complementary capabilities rather than independent catalogs.

Texcel

Hose, hydraulics and fluid-conveyance products create a strong entry point into industrial, distribution and OEM applications.

SHF

Stainless fittings and adapters expand the platform deeper into demanding fluid-transfer, instrumentation and corrosive applications.

OmegaOne

Manufactured stainless components add another technical capability that can support broader engineered solutions.

APG

Sealing, gasketing and coupling capabilities create additional opportunities to own more of the customer's fluid-conveyance system.

CROSS-BRAND GROWTH
The Questions I Would Want Answered

Customer Overlap

Which customers currently purchase from one brand but not the other platform companies?

Product Penetration

Where are existing Texcel customers purchasing fittings, couplings and sealing products today?

Distributor Opportunity

Which distributors already trust one platform brand and could logically expand into additional product categories?

Share of Wallet

Where can the organization own more of the customer's bill of material instead of competing for individual component orders?

ROLE SCALE

Protect the Base Before Redesigning It

The South region is already approximately $25 million and the largest Texcel region. My first responsibility would be to understand and protect that base before deciding what needs to change.

Understand the Economics

  • Revenue by customer
  • Revenue by product family
  • Gross margin and mix
  • Customer concentration
  • Territory performance

Find Untapped Growth

  • Share-of-wallet opportunities
  • Lost or dormant business
  • Cross-brand opportunities
  • Strategic distributor penetration
  • Selected vertical-market plays
EXECUTION

First 90 Days

Days 1–30 | Learn

  • Meet every salesperson individually
  • Travel in the field with the team
  • Meet key distributors and customers
  • Learn the major product families
  • Understand operations and customer service
  • Review revenue, margin and pipeline

Days 31–60 | Prioritize

  • Identify the highest-value growth pools
  • Map account and territory opportunity
  • Assess pipeline quality and coverage
  • Identify talent or coverage gaps
  • Address obvious quick wins

Days 61–90 | Execute

  • Establish operating cadence
  • Set clear expectations and scorecards
  • Launch focused growth initiatives
  • Improve forecast credibility
  • Align resources around major opportunities

Leadership Principle

I want the first changes I make to be based on evidence, not ego. Listen first, understand the economics and the people, then act.

LONG-TERM VALUE CREATION
Three-Year Growth Framework

Year 1 | Build the Foundation

Protect the existing business, improve forecast credibility, establish territory and account plans, understand product penetration and create a short list of measurable growth priorities.

Year 2 | Prove Repeatable Plays

Double down on the initiatives that produce measurable results: cross-selling, competitor conversions, distributor expansion and selected vertical markets.

Year 3 | Scale

Scale the successful plays, develop leadership underneath the organization and create a repeatable multi-brand commercial model.

The Goal

Build profitable growth that is not dependent on one salesperson, one customer or one product line — a commercial system that can continue to scale.

VERTICAL GROWTH THESIS

Data Center Cooling

One example of how I would turn platform capability into a focused sales initiative.

Data-center cooling is attractive because the opportunity is larger than an individual hose or fitting. Cooling systems require fluid conveyance, connections, couplings and sealing — creating an opportunity to attack the application across several platform capabilities.

Where to Hunt

  • Chiller manufacturers
  • CDU manufacturers
  • Cooling-skid builders
  • Mechanical contractors
  • System integrators
  • Industrial distributors

Commercial Objective

Get specified into the system and understand how much of the complete cooling package the platform can supply — rather than competing only for replacement components.

And where does it need the most leadership or support today?
